Privacy & data

Your information should stay yours.

This policy explains what BorderGuide collects, why we use it, and how we protect the information you share while planning your journey.

BorderGuide collects only the information needed to provide, secure, and improve our services. We do not use your private planning data outside the purposes described below.

1. Information we collect

BorderGuide collects information to provide better services to all our users. We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, subscribe to our newsletter, use our AI relocation planning features, or contact us for support.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve our services. This includes personalising features such as custom itineraries, setting appropriate cost estimates, communicating with you, and protecting BorderGuide and our users.

Your itinerary and planning data is handled securely to provide relevant guidance and insights.

3. Data security

We work to protect BorderGuide and our users from unauthorised access to, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of information we hold. Documents and IDs uploaded into our secure storage ecosystem are encrypted in transit and at rest.

4. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your uploaded documents, or your privacy rights, email privacy@borderguide.app.
